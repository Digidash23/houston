- Hanken Grotesk (hanken-grotesk-latin.woff2)
  Copyright The Hanken Grotesk Project Authors (https://github.com/Rajdhani-Fonts/Hanken-Grotesk)

  A variable font (weight axis 100–900), latin subset, retrieved from Google Fonts
  (fonts.gstatic.com). We ship the latin subset only (the marketing landing page is
  English); the es/pt subpages do not use this face. The landing declares the 300–800
  range of the axis and drives the whole page from this single humanist grotesque — a
  light 300 display, 400 body, 600 UI — approximating Mercury's airy low-contrast
  display voice for the partnership prototype.

- Inter (inter-latin.woff2)
